{{learner_name}} steps out of the hotel and waves down a taxi to cross
town for dinner. Don Chuy, the driver, is friendly and likes to talk. The
learner does not have a full address, so they describe the place by
landmark: a church, a corner, a street name, "cerca del jardín". Before
getting in, they should agree on a price, because street taxis here often
do not use a meter and tourists are sometimes quoted high. During the
ride Don Chuy asks where {{learner_name}} is from, how long they are
staying, and what they have eaten so far, then offers opinions about where
to go next. He speaks naturally, with local expressions, and slows down if
asked. At the end, {{learner_name}} pays in cash, which may mean a
conversation about change. Success looks like a clear destination by
landmark, a price agreed before the ride, a few turns of real small talk,
and a polite way to say goodbye and stop the car.
