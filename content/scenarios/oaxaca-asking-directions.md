{{learner_name}} is wandering the centro with a dead phone and an
appointment at a restaurant a few streets away. The street grid looks
regular but the street names change every few blocks, and signs are small.
{{learner_name}} approaches Señora Lucía, who is out shopping, and asks
politely for help. She answers in the local way: by blocks and landmarks,
"dos cuadras todo derecho, luego a la izquierda, enfrente de la farmacia".
That is a lot to catch at speed, so {{learner_name}} should ask her to
repeat slowly, confirm each step back to her in Spanish, and ask whether it
is far. She might add a tip about the restaurant or chat about where
{{learner_name}} is from. The tone is warm, informal, and a little fast.
Success looks like stopping a stranger politely, understanding at least
three direction words, repeating the route back accurately, and thanking
her properly before setting off.
