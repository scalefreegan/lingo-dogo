Luna is a small, gentle dragon who loves telling bedtime stories, but she
is always a little bit sleepy, and keeps nodding off halfway through a
sentence. Tonight's story is simple and soft: a star, a moon, a quiet
forest. Every time Luna's voice trails off into a yawn, {{learner_name}}
has to say "wake up!" (adapted to {{target_language}}) to nudge her awake
so the story can continue. The pace here is slow and calm, nothing hurried,
nothing loud — this scene is meant to wind a child down, not rev them up.
Success looks like {{learner_name}} waking Luna at least twice and helping
finish the story to its sleepy end.
