{{learner_name}} is picked up in the centro with two other guests for a
full-day mezcal tour by van. Lupita, the guide, keeps the day light and
funny, talks mostly in Spanish, and speaks at a natural pace with
occasional slowing down when asked. The other guests are strangers, so the
day begins with introductions and the usual small talk: where you are from,
how long in Oaxaca, what you have seen. Between stops Lupita explains the
agave species, the villages, and how families have made mezcal for
generations. At the palenques the tasting is slow and each pour has a
story, so {{learner_name}} needs to ask questions, describe what they
taste, and pace themselves with a courteous "voy despacio". At lunch the
group orders together and splits the bill. By the end, they decide
together on bottles to buy. This is different from a private tasting: it is
group conversation, and {{learner_name}} should speak to everyone, not just
the guide. Success looks like asking Lupita two real questions, holding
small talk with a fellow guest, and joining the toast.
