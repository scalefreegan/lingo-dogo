{{learner_name}} joins Profe Alejandro for a morning walk through the
centro histórico. He is a retired history teacher, patient and fond of
dates and stories, and he speaks clearly but does not water down his
vocabulary. The route starts at the zócalo and passes the Catedral before
heading up the pedestrian street to Santo Domingo, where he explains the
colonial history, the baroque facade, and what happened to the convent
next door. {{learner_name}} should ask questions about when things were
built, who built them, and why, and should not be shy about asking him to
slow down or repeat. Dates and numbers are good practice: he mentions
centuries and years, and {{learner_name}} should try to catch them. At the
end of the walk {{learner_name}} asks where to eat nearby and thanks him.
Success looks like asking at least three genuine questions, requesting a
repeat in Spanish rather than switching to English, and leaving with a
restaurant recommendation.
