{{learner_name}} has just landed at Oaxaca's small airport after a long
day of travel. The first stop is immigration, where a brisk but polite
officer asks the usual questions: the reason for the trip, how long the
stay will be, and where {{learner_name}} is staying. Keep these short and
formal. Then comes the bag, the customs button at the exit, and the
arrivals hall, where several voices are offering a ride. The sensible move
is the authorized taxi counter, which sells tickets by zone rather than by
meter. The clerk is efficient: where to, how many, how many bags. The
goal is a ticket to the centro histórico at the right price, with the
destination confirmed so there are no surprises. {{learner_name}} is tired
and may need things repeated, which the dojo master should do patiently
but at natural speed. Success looks like answering the immigration
questions in full sentences, asking about the zone and price, and leaving
with a ticket and a clear idea of where to find the taxi.
