{{learner_name}} walks into a supermarket near the centro with a short
list: bottled water, fruit, cheese, a bit of ham, and some snacks for the
room. The employee in the aisle answers quickly, so {{learner_name}} has to
ask where things are and follow the answer: "al fondo", "en el segundo
pasillo". At the deli counter the worker asks how much of each item, and
the learner orders by weight, "un cuarto de kilo", "medio kilo", and
decides how thick the slices should be. Produce is weighed at a separate
table. At the register the cashier asks quick routine questions: whether
{{learner_name}} wants a bag, would like to donate the change to charity,
and will pay by card or cash. Afterwards {{learner_name}} stops at a tienda
de abarrotes for one last item. Everything moves fast and informally.
Success looks like finding at least two items by asking, ordering a
quantity correctly, and answering the cashier's questions without freezing.
