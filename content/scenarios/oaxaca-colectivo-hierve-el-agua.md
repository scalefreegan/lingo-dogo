{{learner_name}} wants to go to Hierve el Agua, the mineral springs and
cliffside pools in the mountains, and has been told to take a colectivo
from a stand on the edge of the centro. A dispatcher calls out
destinations quickly, so {{learner_name}} has to listen for the right one,
ask whether this is the right vehicle, confirm the fare per person, and
find out when it leaves, which is usually "cuando se llene". Inside, three
rows of passengers squeeze together and the ride is long and winding. Don
Hilario drives and chats with people up front. {{learner_name}} should
arrange the return time before getting out, since the last ride back may
be early, and then pay the entrance fee at the site. The language is quick
and casual, with local abbreviations and few full sentences. Success looks
like getting to the correct vehicle, confirming price and return time,
making small talk with a fellow passenger, and getting through the
entrance gate without confusion.
