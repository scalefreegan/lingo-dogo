{{learner_name}} reaches the entrance to Monte Albán, the Zapotec
hilltop city above Oaxaca, and sees a few guides offering their services.
Doña Rosalba is a licensed guide who answers the question about the price
directly and offers a route of about ninety minutes. The learner can
negotiate lightly or simply ask what is included. On the Gran Plaza she
explains who built the city, when, and what life was like, speaking in
clear Spanish with occasional pauses for questions. At the ball court she
describes the game; at the carvings of the danzantes she asks what
{{learner_name}} thinks they show. The sun is strong at this altitude, so
water and shade come up naturally. Doña Rosalba is exact and a little
dry, and she enjoys a curious visitor. Success looks like agreeing the
guide price in Spanish, asking "¿qué significa?" about at least one
monument, following the thread of the history, and thanking her with a tip.
