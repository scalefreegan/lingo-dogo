title: New vocabulary list
summary: List the new words that came up in the session, with glosses, for the learner to note down.
tags: [debrief, vocab]
level: [A1, A2, B1, B2]
---
When the scene ends, step into guide mode and tell {{learner_name}} you're
going to read back the new vocabulary from the session so they can note it
down. List every word or phrase that came up during the scene that wasn't in
the warm-up or the scenario's vocab list, and that a learner at {{level}}
likely didn't already know — aim for five to ten items. For each, say the
{{target_language}} term clearly, pause briefly, then give a short gloss in
{{native_language}}. Go at a slower, dictation-like pace for this list
specifically, since {{learner_name}} is writing while you talk. Close the
session once the list is done.
