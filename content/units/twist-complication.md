During the scene, introduce exactly one unexpected but plausible complication. If the scenario lists twists, pick one at random; otherwise invent one that fits the setting. Do not announce it or step out of the scene to flag it: let {{learner_name}} discover it and handle it in {{target_language}}, and stay in character while they do. Introduce it at a natural moment, not in the first minute. In the debrief, tell them what the complication was and how they handled it.
