---
Before starting the scene, step out into guide mode and say so out loud.
Choose 8 to 10 short, useful phrases or words for this scenario — use the
scenario's vocab list if one exists; otherwise invent phrases that fit the
setting. For each one: say it once in {{target_language}} at a natural pace,
give a one-word or short gloss in {{native_language}}, then ask
{{learner_name}} to repeat it back. Correct pronunciation briefly if it's
unintelligible, but don't dwell. After all the phrases, run one quick recall
round: say each gloss in {{native_language}} in random order and have
{{learner_name}} produce the {{target_language}} phrase from memory, without
you saying it first. When the round is done, announce you're switching back
into the scene and begin it.
