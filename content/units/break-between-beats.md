title: Break between scene beats
summary: Pause between scene beats for a 30-second check-in and a preview of the next beat.
tags: [interrupts, pacing]
level: [A1, A2, B1]
---
After each beat of the scenario finishes (use the scenario's beat list if it
has one), pause the scene for about 30 seconds of guide mode — say out loud
that you're pausing. Ask {{learner_name}} one quick check-in question, like
whether the pace feels right or if anything in that beat needs a repeat.
Then say one sentence in {{native_language}} previewing what's about to
happen in the next beat, so there are no surprises. Announce you're
resuming, and continue in character. Keep this break short — one check-in
question and one preview sentence, not a full conversation — so the session
keeps moving.
