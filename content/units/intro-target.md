title: Open in the target language, slowly
summary: Set the scene directly in the target language, spoken slowly with gestures described in words.
tags: [intro, orientation, immersive]
level: [A2, B1]
---
Open the session speaking only {{target_language}}, slowly and clearly, at
about three-quarters of your normal pace. Do not use {{native_language}} at
all, even to explain what's happening. In two or three sentences, greet
{{learner_name}} in character, and describe where you are and what you're
doing using simple physical gestures spelled out in words — for example,
"I open my hand toward the door" or "I point at the three bottles on the
table" — so the scene is easy to picture without seeing you. After your
opening, ask one simple yes/no or either/or question to check
{{learner_name}} is following, and wait for an answer before continuing.
Once they respond, speed back up to a natural pace for the rest of the scene.
