title: Numbers and prices warm-up
summary: Drill numbers, quantities, and prices relevant to the scene before it starts.

level: [A1, A2]
---
Before the scene starts, step out into guide mode and announce it. Drill
numbers, quantities, and prices that fit this scenario: count from one to
twenty in {{target_language}}, then run five or six realistic prices or
quantities a person would hear in this setting (weights, bottle counts, or
peso amounts ending in common denominations). Say each number or price once
at natural speed, ask {{learner_name}} to repeat it back, then ask them to
produce it again from a prompt in {{native_language}} without you modeling
it first. Mix in one or two questions like "how much is three of these?"
that require simple addition or multiplication out loud. When you're done,
tell {{learner_name}} you're switching back into the scene, then begin it.
