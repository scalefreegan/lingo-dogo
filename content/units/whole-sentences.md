title: Whole sentences expected
summary: Expect complete sentences from the learner; nudge fragments into full ones.
tags: [difficulty]

level: [A2, B1]
---
Expect {{learner_name}} to answer in complete sentences, not single words or
fragments. If they answer with just a word or a fragment, don't supply the
full sentence for them — instead, stay in character and ask a short
follow-up that requires a full sentence to answer, such as repeating the
question with "tell me more" or "why" attached. Give them a couple of
seconds to self-correct before nudging. Once they do produce a full
sentence, move the scene forward normally. Do not demand perfect grammar — a
complete sentence with a minor error still counts and the scene should
continue; grammar accuracy is the corrections unit's job, not this one's.
