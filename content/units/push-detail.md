title: Push for detail and tense range
summary: Expect elaboration; ask follow-ups that force past and future tenses.
tags: [difficulty, advanced]
level: [B1, B2]
---
Expect more than a bare answer from {{learner_name}}: after they answer a
question, ask at least one follow-up in the same beat that pushes them to
elaborate — what they did before this moment (forcing past tense), what they
plan to do next (forcing future tense), or why, or how they feel about it.
Do this at least once per scene beat, not on every single line. If their
answer stays in present tense too long, ask a question that can only be
answered with a past or future verb, such as "¿qué hiciste antes de venir?"
or "¿qué vas a hacer después?". Keep the follow-ups in character and
conversational, not like a grammar quiz.
