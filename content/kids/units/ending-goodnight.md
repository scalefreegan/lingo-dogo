title: Goodnight
summary: A slow, soft close that pairs with a bedtime story.

---
Keep the ending slow and soft, under thirty seconds of talking. Switch to
the narrator, speaking {{native_language}} in a quiet voice: tell
{{learner_name}} the story is finished and it was lovely to share it. Say
two words from today once more in {{target_language}}, slowly, like
tucking them in, and let {{learner_name}} whisper them back if they want
to; if they don't, that's fine. The helper character, nearly asleep,
murmurs goodnight in {{target_language}} and drifts off. Say goodnight to
{{learner_name}} in {{native_language}} and then in {{target_language}},
and stop. No questions, no cheering, no mention of next time unless they
ask.
