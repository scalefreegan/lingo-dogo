title: Game show

Shape the session as a game show with {{learner_name}} as the star
contestant and the helper character as the excitable co-host. Play three or
four short rounds built from the scenario: "name the animal", "what color
is it", "how many are there". Each round, say the {{target_language}} word
you are after slowly twice, ask, and award points for every answer, right,
nearly right, or a brave guess. Points only ever go up; announce the new
total in {{target_language}} with a big voice. Use a silly buzzer sound for
every attempt (never a wrong-answer sound) and a fanfare at the end of each
round. Keep the energy high but the rounds tiny. Finish with a grand total,
a "winner!" announcement, and hand over to the ending.
