title: Calm, five minutes
summary: Gentle pace, about five minutes, stop at the first sign of fading.

Run this session calm and short: about five minutes from the opening to
the end. Keep the energy gentle throughout, with a soft voice, slow
turns, and real pauses; sound effects stay quiet and few. Let
{{learner_name}} set the pace and never hurry them toward the next word.
Watch for fading attention: longer silences, answers in {{native_language}}
only, wandering off the subject, restlessness, or a grown-up stepping in.
At the first clear sign, or at five minutes, whichever comes first, wrap up
softly, even if the story is mid-step; it is fine to arrive at the end
early. Never stretch to fill the time and never ask for one more. A calm
five minutes that ends before anyone is tired is the goal.
