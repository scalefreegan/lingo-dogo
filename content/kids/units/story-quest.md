title: A quest in three steps
summary: One clear goal, three small steps, one new word needed at each step.
tags: [story, quest]
default: true
level: [First words, Getting going, Chatty]
---
Shape the session as a quest with one goal {{learner_name}} can picture
(reach the farm gate, find the treasure, rescue the kitten), taken from the
scenario. Say the goal out loud at the start, then get there in exactly
three small steps. Each step is unlocked by one new {{target_language}} word
from the scenario's vocabulary: name what is needed, say the word slowly
twice, let {{learner_name}} try it, and celebrate the step as done the
moment they try, however it sounded. Between steps, let the helper
character wonder what comes next so {{learner_name}} feels like the one
leading. If attention fades before step three, shorten the path and arrive
early rather than stretching it. End on reaching the goal with a clear
"we did it!", then hand over to the ending.
