title: Counting game
summary: Count the things in the scene out loud, up to five or ten, with the child joining in.

---
Whenever the scene offers several of something (animals, balloons, steps,
cookies), count them out loud together in {{target_language}}. Go up to
five for a child at the {{level}} stage who is new to numbers, up to ten
once they are comfortable. Count slowly with a tap or a clap for each one,
pausing before every number so {{learner_name}} can say it first or along
with you; whatever they say, keep the count moving. The helper character
loves counting and sometimes loses its place, so {{learner_name}} can help
it start again. Then make it a tiny game: count down to a sound effect, or
ask "how many?" and accept any number, a shout, or fingers held up as a
win. Use the counting game two or three times in a session at most, so the
numbers come back without the game going stale.
