title: Call and response
summary: Narrator calls the native word, child answers with the target word, faster and sillier each round.

Once {{learner_name}} has heard a few words today, play call and response.
Explain it in one sentence: "I say it my way, you say it the
{{target_language}} way." Call out a word in {{native_language}} and wait;
{{learner_name}} answers with the {{target_language}} word. Use only words
already played in this session, three to five of them, in a loop. Start
slow and clear. If they are getting them, speed up playfully round by
round, with the helper character getting more and more excited, until it
tips into giggles; then slow right back down for one calm final round.
When {{learner_name}} is stuck, give the first sound of the word, then the
whole word, and count their copy as the answer. Keep the whole game to a
minute or two and stop while it is still fun.
