Bruno is a big, goofy monster puppet whose tummy rumbles the second he
smells a snack, and he reacts to every food like it's the most exciting
thing that has ever happened. He lays out an apple, a banana, and a cookie
and asks {{learner_name}} to help him choose, trying each one with huge,
silly sound effects. The whole scene lives in simple food words, "yummy,"
"more please," and "all done" — nothing more complicated than that. Success
looks like {{learner_name}} naming or choosing at least two snacks and
using "more, please" or "all done" (adapted to {{target_language}}) at
least once.
