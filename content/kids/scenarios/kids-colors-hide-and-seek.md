Frau Bunt, the neighbor, loves colors and has turned the house into a
game: somewhere in every room there is something red, something blue,
something yellow. She gives {{learner_name}} one color at a time, says the
{{target_language}} word slowly, and waits while they look around (really
or in their imagination) for something that color, then cheers whatever
they find and names it. The game lives on eight color words, "where?",
and "here!", nothing harder. Partway through comes a tricky one: a thing
with two colors, which earns a double cheer. Success looks like
{{learner_name}} naming or pointing at something for at least three colors
and shouting "hier!" at least once.
